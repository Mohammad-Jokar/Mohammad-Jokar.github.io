## Selected Research Projects

### Global Antimicrobial Resistance in Urinary Tract Infections

A systematic review and data-driven assessment of global antimicrobial resistance prevalence and temporal trends among major urinary pathogens, with comparisons across geographic regions, country income groups, age groups, and antimicrobial classes.

### Large Language Models in Veterinary Emergency Triage

An expert assessment of the safety, accuracy, helpfulness, and communication quality of public large language model chatbots in emergency scenarios involving dogs and cats.

### AI-Assisted Systematic Reviews

Development and evaluation of retrieval-augmented generation and large language model pipelines for literature screening, data extraction, quality assessment, evidence synthesis, and continuously updated living systematic reviews.

### Infodemiology and Risk Communication

Analysis of public search behaviour and online information patterns relating to antimicrobial resistance, vaccination, emerging infectious diseases, and public health risk communication.

### Veterinary Education and Immersive Technologies

Development and evaluation of artificial intelligence, virtual reality, mixed reality, and sensor-integrated educational tools for veterinary and comparative anatomy training.
