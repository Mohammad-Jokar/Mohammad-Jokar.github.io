# Mohammad Jokar, DVM

## About Me

I am a Curriculum Mapping Coordinator and veterinary researcher at the University of Calgary. My academic work focuses on antimicrobial resistance, One Health, veterinary epidemiology, systematic reviews, health data science, infodemiology, and artificial intelligence.

My research applies evidence synthesis, meta-analysis, large language models, machine learning, and interactive data visualization to challenges affecting human and animal health.

## Selected Research Projects

### Global Antimicrobial Resistance in Urinary Tract Infections

A systematic review and data-driven assessment of global antimicrobial resistance prevalence and temporal trends among major urinary pathogens.

### Large Language Models in Veterinary Emergency Triage

An expert assessment of the safety, accuracy, helpfulness, and communication quality of public large language model chatbots in emergency scenarios involving dogs and cats.

### AI-Assisted Systematic Reviews

Development and evaluation of retrieval-augmented generation and large language model pipelines for screening, data extraction, quality assessment, and living systematic reviews.

### Infodemiology and Risk Communication

Analysis of public search behavior relating to antimicrobial resistance, vaccination, emerging infections, and risk communication.
