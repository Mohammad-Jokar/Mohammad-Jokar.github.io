# About Me

I am a veterinarian and researcher at the University of Calgary working across veterinary medicine, public health, epidemiology, evidence synthesis, and health data science.

My research focuses on antimicrobial resistance, One Health, systematic reviews, infodemiology, artificial intelligence, large language models, and interactive data visualization.

My work aims to transform complex health data into accessible evidence that can support surveillance, clinical decision-making, risk communication, education, and public health policy.

## Research Identity

My interdisciplinary research connects:

- Veterinary medicine
- Antimicrobial resistance
- Human and animal health
- One Health
- Epidemiology
- Evidence synthesis
- Artificial intelligence
- Health data visualization
