# Mohammad Jokar, DVM

## Veterinary Researcher in Antimicrobial Resistance, One Health, and Health Data Science

I am a veterinarian and researcher at the University of Calgary working at the intersection of antimicrobial resistance, One Health, veterinary epidemiology, evidence synthesis, health data science, and artificial intelligence.

My research applies systematic reviews, meta-analysis, infodemiology, machine learning, large language models, and interactive data visualization to challenges affecting human and animal health.

## Research Focus

- Antimicrobial resistance and antimicrobial stewardship
- One Health and veterinary epidemiology
- Systematic reviews and evidence synthesis
- Artificial intelligence and large language models in health
- Infodemiology and digital disease surveillance
- Health data analysis and visualization
- Veterinary and health-professions education

## Featured Projects

### Global Antimicrobial Resistance in Urinary Tract Infections

A systematic review and data-driven assessment of resistance prevalence and temporal trends among major urinary pathogens.

### Large Language Models in Veterinary Emergency Triage

An expert assessment of the safety, accuracy, helpfulness, and communication quality of public chatbot responses to emergency scenarios involving dogs and cats.

### AI-Assisted Evidence Synthesis

Development and evaluation of retrieval-augmented generation pipelines for screening, data extraction, quality assessment, and living systematic reviews.
