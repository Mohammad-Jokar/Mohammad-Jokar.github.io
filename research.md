# Research

## Antimicrobial Resistance in Urinary Tract Infections

A global systematic review and data-driven evaluation of antimicrobial resistance prevalence and temporal trends among urinary pathogens.

The project combines:

- Systematic review and meta-analysis
- Antimicrobial resistance surveillance
- Temporal trend analysis
- Geographic comparisons
- Interactive data visualization
- Comparison with international surveillance systems

## Large Language Models in Veterinary Emergency Triage

An expert assessment of the quality and safety of public large language model chatbot responses to emergency scenarios involving dogs and cats.

The assessment examines:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Triage recommendations
- Safety
- Helpfulness
- Communication quality

## AI-Assisted Systematic Reviews

Development and evaluation of retrieval-augmented generation and large language model pipelines for:

- Title and abstract screening
- Full-text screening
- Data extraction
- Quality and risk-of-bias assessment
- Evidence synthesis
- Living systematic-review updates

## Infodemiology and Risk Communication

Analysis of online information-seeking behaviour related to antimicrobial resistance, vaccination, infectious diseases, and emerging health threats.

## Veterinary and One Health Research

Additional research addresses veterinary epidemiology, animal health, emerging infections, veterinary education, and One Health.
